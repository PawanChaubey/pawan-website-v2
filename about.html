I am Dr. Pawan Kumar Chaubey, a climate scientist working on hydroclimatic extremes, climate variability, Earth observation and climate risk.

My work combines observations, climate models, statistical approaches and geospatial analysis to understand changing climate hazards and their implications for water resources, infrastructure and climate resilience.

I currently work at the Earth Observatory of Singapore, Nanyang Technological University, Singapore.
