ABOUT

Understanding climate extremes and translating climate information into useful knowledge.

My research focuses on hydroclimate extremes across South and Southeast Asia, with emphasis on drought, extreme precipitation, climate variability and decision-relevant climate information.

I combine observations, climate models, statistical methods, geospatial analysis and Earth-system information to investigate changing climate risks.
