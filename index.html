About

Research at the intersection of climate, extremes and risk.

Dr. Pawan Kumar Chaubey is a climate scientist working on hydroclimatic extremes, climate variability, Earth observation and climate risk. His research combines observations, climate models, statistical methods and geospatial analysis to understand climate-related risks across South and Southeast Asia.
